# Prompt

## Base name
portrait_treskal_farmer_female_001

## Prompt usado

Create a canonical Nimroel NPC portrait asset. Image A is only a general world-context reference for Nimroel; use it only as loose setting context and do not copy any map graphics or labels into the image.

Generate a vertical 4:5 medium-bust portrait of a female peasant from Treskal, in Norgard. This is a portrait asset, not a full scene illustration. Show head, neck, shoulders, and upper torso from about mid-chest upward, with a little space above the head. The woman should appear as a distinct individual from the world of Nimroel, not a generic fantasy peasant.

Resolved individual variation for this portrait: female, apparent age about 44, adult, robust but natural build, broad-oval face, slightly strong jaw, modest cheek fullness, a larger practical nose, subtle facial asymmetry, sun-touched skin with visible texture and fine lines from outdoor work, brown eyes, thick practical brows, dark ash-brown hair with a few early grey strands, hair loosely tied back with some escaped strands, no glamorous styling, no modern grooming.

Expression: calm, serious, a little tired, humane and grounded, with a hint of quiet resilience. She should look like someone who works the land every day.

Clothing and presentation: peasant work clothing appropriate to Treskal and Norgard, preindustrial and functional, with natural layered fabrics. Use an aged off-white or bone-colored linen undergarment/shirt with a muted earthy overdress or bodice/apron in brown, faded green, grey-brown, or similar subdued rural tones. Presentation state level 2: she is in the middle of a workday or has just been working. Clothing patina grade C leaning toward D: clearly used everyday garments, not pristine. Show believable earth stains, dust, and wear especially on the visible apron, neckline, shoulders, and fabric edges. She may be reasonably washed in the face, but the clothing must still show the memory of work. Avoid pure white fabric and avoid spotless clothing.

Background: softly blurred contextual rural Treskal background only, suggesting farmland or vineyard-adjacent countryside in a mild, somewhat humid Norgard setting, with hints of timber structures or cultivated fields. Keep it unobtrusive and non-narrative.

Style and quality rules: grounded cinematic naturalism, realistic anatomy, physically believable materials, preindustrial fantasy with no modern elements, no decorative fantasy excess, no pin-up look, no fashion-model face, no airbrushed skin, no exaggerated beauty. Make her clearly look like a real woman of this world. The portrait should feel reusable for an RPG dialogue or character interface.
