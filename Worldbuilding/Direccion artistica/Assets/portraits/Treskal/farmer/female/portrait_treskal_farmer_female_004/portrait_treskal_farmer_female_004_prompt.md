## Prompt usado
Create a canonical NPC portrait for Nimroel: a female farmer from the Treskal area of Norgard. Follow all applicable visual sources: the Global Visual Bible, the NPC Portrait Visual Bible, the Norgard visual profile, the local Treskal portrait visual bible, and the portrait diversity registry so the result remains clearly distinct from the previously approved Treskal female farmers.

This should be a realistic, high-detail portrait asset, not an action scene. Depict an adult rural working woman from Treskal in her early to mid 30s, clearly different from the previously approved young, mature, and elderly campesinas. She should appear physically sturdy and healthy, with a robust build visible in the shoulders and upper torso, a broad rounded face, light sun-touched skin with natural redness and subtle texture, and a calm, self-possessed expression. Give her curly auburn to chestnut hair, partially tucked under a light linen headscarf, with loose curls framing the face. Eyes grey-green or muted hazel.

Clothing should be humble, practical and regionally grounded: an off-white linen blouse and a dark brown, visibly worn work bodice or overdress, with believable dirt, creasing and labor wear. Presentation state: 2 (working day / active labor). Clothing patina: C (normal everyday use, well-worn by labor). Dirt and wear should be plausible and localized on work garments.

Frame as a high-quality chest-up portrait in soft natural daylight with shallow depth of field. The background must be contextual and secondary, coherent with both profession and the Treskal local bible: cultivated kitchen-garden rows or vegetable beds, rustic wooden fencing, a stone-and-wood rural building with slate roof, nearby trees, and a suggestion of river or water in the landscape. Keep the subject dominant and sharply readable. No modern elements, no glamour styling, no studio backdrop, and no exaggerated fantasy costume.
