# Prompt

Create a diegetic fantasy map for the world of Nimroel: an official city plan of Treskal, intended to exist in-world as a public-administrative document from the House Valrik administration. Use a 16:10 landscape format suitable for in-game use.

Use the broader Nimroel / Treskal regional context only to stay coherent with the world, coastline feeling and regional atmosphere, not as a strict tracing source.

Render the final image as an elegant parchment map in a hand-illustrated ink-and-watercolor style, richly detailed but readable. The city should feel believable and grounded, yet slightly romanticized rather than mechanically exact. It must look like the first real official map artifact of Nimroel.

Requirements:
- Main title in Spanish: "PLANO OFICIAL DE LA CIUDAD DE TRESKAL"
- Subtitle: "ADMINISTRACIÓN DE LA CASA VALRIK"
- Include a heraldic crest for Casa Valrik in the title area.
- Include a compass rose with north marker and a scale bar.
- The map should show the river, the coast, the Puente de los Gemelos, the main city mass on the east bank, the civil port, and the Royal shipyard / main naval base area.
- Distinguish restricted royal/naval areas from the ordinary public city, but do not reveal excessive military detail.
- Use Spanish labels, cleanly rendered and legible, including: "Periferia de Abastecimiento", "Ganado", "Campo de Instrucción", "Cuartel Territorial", "Puente de los Gemelos", "Barrios Residenciales", "Carpinteros y Talleres", "Sede de Casa Valrik", "Administración Territorial", "Justicia", "Mercado Principal", "Guardia Urbana", "Lonja de Pescado", "Puerto Civil", "Astilleros Reales", "Base Naval Principal", and a small legend note indicating "Zona de acceso restringido (Administración Real)".
- Show the sea labeled "Mar de Suthiros".
- Decorative border and official-cartouche layout are welcome.
- The result should read as an official public city map of Treskal, beautiful, believable, and suitable for lore and in-game display.
