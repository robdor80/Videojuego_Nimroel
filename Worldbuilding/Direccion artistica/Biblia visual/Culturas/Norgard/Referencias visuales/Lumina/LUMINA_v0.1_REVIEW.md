# LUMINA_v0.1_REVIEW

## General assessment
`LUMINA_v0.1_REFERENCE_001.png` works well as a **first canonical visual reference** for Lumina.

## What works well
- **Identity achieved:** the settlement reads immediately as a **small, beautiful wine town** rather than a major city.
- **Regional coherence:** materials and palette fit the broader **central Norgard** family while differentiating Lumina from colder northern settlements.
- **Compactness:** the town feels **small, compact and cohesive**, exactly as intended.
- **Wine character:** vineyards, bodegas, carts, barrels and market activity clearly establish the settlement’s **vinicultural identity**.
- **Pleasant visual tone:** the image feels **coqueta, bonita y atractiva**, with a welcoming quality.
- **Topographic cue:** the plateau/mesa-edge context is present and reasonably legible.
- **No major river:** the composition respects the rule that Lumina is **not** defined by a strong river.
- **Central social core:** the open central square reads as an important communal and commercial heart.

## Small reservations / possible future refinement
- One taller rear building reads slightly like a **chapel or civic bell-tower**. If desired later, a future version could reduce any religious reading and make it feel more purely civic.
- Some vegetation and hillside rhythms give a slightly **southern vineyard-country** feel. This is attractive and coherent, but if needed later it can be tuned further toward a more specifically Norgard interpretation.
- The first image succeeds mainly as an **establishing view**. A future `v0.2` could focus more directly on the **central square and annual wine-market identity**.

## Canon decision for now
Approved as:
- **`LUMINA_v0.1_REFERENCE_001.png`**

Recommended status:
- **Valid as first operational canonical reference** until a possible future refinement.
