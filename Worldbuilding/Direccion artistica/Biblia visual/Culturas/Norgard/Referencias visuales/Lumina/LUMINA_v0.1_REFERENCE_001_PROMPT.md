## Prompt used
Create a new canonical visual reference for **Lumina**, a small beautiful wine-producing settlement in central Norgard, Nimroel.

**Image A** is the map crop showing Lumina’s placement at the entrance of a raised mesa/plateau near Dorth and Karsath; use it as geographic grounding only.  
**Image B** is the canonical Dorth reference; use it as a style and material reference for regional architecture only, not as a composition template.  
**Image C** is the global Nimroel visual reference; use it for the overall Nimroel mood and visual language.

Lumina should be shown in a **wide establishing exterior view**, beautiful and visually attractive, with the viewer seeing the whole compact settlement and its surrounding wine landscape. The town sits at the entrance to a plateau or mesa-like elevated area. Make that landform legible: Lumina nestles near the edge or entry of this flat elevated terrain, with vineyards spreading nearby to the north and south across surrounding slopes and open land, but the vineyards do **not** begin immediately against every house. There should be roads and paths radiating from the settlement into the vineyard country.

Lumina is **smaller than Dorth** and much smaller than Arleon. It is compact, coqueta, prosperous, tidy and charming rather than monumental. **No walls or fortifications.** Architecture: light stone and timber construction, **red clay tile roofs**, neat and well-kept **cobbled streets and plazas**. The settlement has a recognizably beautiful **elongated central square**, relatively large for the settlement because it hosts the annual Lumina wine market. The square should feel organic and important. Around the square, the buildings are slightly larger and more representative than the rest of the town but they should not dwarf the rest: a local administration building, a grand inn, a prestigious winery or family bodega, merchant houses, restaurants or taverns, all in a harmonious ensemble. Throughout Lumina, show several **smaller family bodegas**, storage buildings, merchant houses and wine-related warehouses rather than one huge industrial complex.

Important geography: **do not show any large river**, no major stream, no canal, no lake. Small rills or hidden rural water sources may exist but they should not define the composition. No harbor. No mountains dominating the town. This is **not** a cold northern settlement and **not** a giant city. The surrounding landscape should show the cultivated wine region, nearby forests, the transition into the plateau or mesa, and a pleasant attractive Norgard countryside.

Make Lumina feel beautiful, inviting and recognizably a wine settlement: stone houses, careful cobbling, warm late-afternoon or early-autumn light, vineyard plots nearby, carts, barrels, wine crates, a few townsfolk, and a tranquil lived-in atmosphere. **No heraldic banners or flags. No exaggerated fantasy elements.** Realistic cinematic naturalism, detailed, **16:9**.
