## Prompt resuelto

```text
Wide cinematic establishing view of Veldora, the main western commercial port city of Norgard in the fantasy world of Nimroel. Naturalistic cinematic realism, physically believable preindustrial medieval city, large, prosperous, active and maritime.

Show a large coastal human city with a vast commercial harbor full of ships, docks, piers, warehouses, cranes or lifting structures appropriate to a preindustrial world, merchants, ship traffic and busy port activity. Veldora is one of the major economic centers of Norgard and the principal maritime gateway for trade with western lands, especially the elven realms across the sea.

The city should feel rich through commerce and volume of activity, not through palatial fantasy excess. Use stone and wood architecture, tiled roofs, dense urban growth, many houses of two or three stories, workshops and businesses on lower floors, richer districts further from the docks, and signs of customs, trade and guild life. The urban fabric should feel large, organically expanded and commercially driven.

Very important geographical cues: show the sea in front, a large forested island directly offshore facing the city, a major river entering or flowing behind the city, and mountain ranges rising in the background inland. The island should feel wooded and substantial, not tiny.

No churches, no religious iconography, no modern industry, no steampunk, no exaggerated fantasy cityscape, no generic Viking port clichés. This is a great human trade port of Norgard: sober, busy, rich and functional.

Daytime, realistic cool-neutral light, broad depth, believable scale, heavy port activity, detailed but grounded materials. Nimroel visual DNA: real, ancient, physical, lived-in, beautiful, restrained.
```
