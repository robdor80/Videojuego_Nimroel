## Prompt resuelto

```text
Wide cinematic establishing view of Arleon, an important regional administrative and logistical city in eastern Norgard, in the fantasy world of Nimroel. Naturalistic cinematic realism, physically believable preindustrial medieval city, orderly, practical and tied to the agricultural wealth of the eastern plains.

Show a medium-to-large unwalled city built near rivers and bridges, serving as the main logistical and administrative center of Norgard’s eastern granary. The city should be surrounded by broad fertile plains, fields, farms, pastures and a river-fed agricultural landscape. Arleon is not a peasant village; it is the organizing hub through which food, grain, livestock and goods are managed and sent westward toward Hallheim.

Architecture should be human, sober and functional: stone and wood buildings, dark slate roofs throughout, warehouses, granaries, stables, corrals, administrative buildings, market areas, carts moving goods, and an urban layout that feels more ordered and efficient than picturesque. The city is not walled. It should feel active, organized and regionally important.

Use an eastern Norgard atmosphere: practical, productive, river-based, and connected to the great agricultural zone. No churches, no temples, no religious symbols, no extravagant fantasy architecture, no generic Viking aesthetic.

Daytime with realistic light, broad sense of space, some traffic and commercial movement, strong agricultural-logistical identity, believable medieval scale. Nimroel visual DNA: real, ancient, physical, lived-in, beautiful, restrained.
```
