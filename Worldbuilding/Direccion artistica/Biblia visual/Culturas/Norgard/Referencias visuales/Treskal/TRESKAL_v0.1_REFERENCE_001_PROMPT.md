## Prompt resuelto

```text
Wide cinematic establishing view of Treskal, a medium-sized human city in eastern Norgard, in the fantasy world of Nimroel. Naturalistic cinematic realism, physically believable preindustrial medieval architecture, lived-in, functional and artisan-centered.

Treskal is famous for its exceptional woodworking and master artisans in timber, furniture and carved wood craftsmanship. Show a city of stone and wood with dark slate roofs, unwalled, connected to river and coastal trade, with visible workshops, timber yards, woodworkers, carpenters, furniture production and refined wooden architectural detailing.

Even ordinary houses should display noticeably superior carpentry: carved beams, brackets, doors, window frames, balconies and structural woodwork. The detailing must look highly skilled but still functional and believable, not fantastically over-ornamented.

The city should feel prosperous through craftsmanship and quality rather than ostentatious wealth. Use sober Norgard visual language: stone, wood, slate, muted colors, medieval realism, practical streets and active trade.

Very important: Treskal belongs to eastern Norgard and should not be surrounded by dramatic mountain ranges. The surrounding landscape should be more open, forest-rich, gently rolling or low-relief, with coast and river influence, not alpine.

No churches, no temples, no religious symbols, no steampunk, no magical glowing decoration, no exaggerated fantasy architecture. Nimroel visual DNA: real, ancient, physical, lived-in, beautiful, restrained.
```
