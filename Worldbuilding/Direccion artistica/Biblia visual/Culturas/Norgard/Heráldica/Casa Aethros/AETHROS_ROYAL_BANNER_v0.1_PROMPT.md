## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical cloth banner of House Aethros for Nimroel, derived directly from the approved AETHROS_HERALDIC_EMBLEM_v0.1.

Use the exact same frontal silver wolf head design as the master emblem. Place it prominently on a vertical hanging banner made from heavy deep royal-blue medieval fabric.

The banner must look physically real and preindustrial:
- thick woven cloth
- visible stitching
- folds and weight
- slight wear from use
- modest fraying near the lower edge
- realistic suspension from a dark metal horizontal pole

The wolf remains silver and frontal, clearly legible at distance. The blue must match the canonical Aethros blue.

Do not redesign the wolf. Do not introduce additional heraldry.

Important:
- no random secondary symbols
- no text
- no slogans
- no crown
- no geometric invented emblems
- no excessive decoration
- no modern synthetic fabric
- no pristine ceremonial perfection

This banner is the single canonical Aethros banner design.

Usage rule:
Visible settlement banners in Norgard are restricted to Hallheim unless a later explicit canon exception is created. Even in Hallheim, use them sparingly and only in royal, administrative, military or ceremonial contexts.

Result target:
A sober, worn but prestigious medieval royal banner carrying the exact canonical frontal silver wolf of House Aethros on deep royal blue cloth.
```
