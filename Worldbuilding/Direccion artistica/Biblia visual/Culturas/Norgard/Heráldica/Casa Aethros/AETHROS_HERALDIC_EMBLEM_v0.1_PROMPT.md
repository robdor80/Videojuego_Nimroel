## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical heraldic emblem of House Aethros for Nimroel.

The emblem must show ONLY the frontal head of a wolf, centered and symmetrical, rendered in aged silver over a deep dark royal blue background.

The wolf must face directly toward the viewer. Do not use a side profile, three-quarter view, full wolf body, running wolf, Stark-like silhouette, or generic heraldic animal pose.

The head should feel powerful, severe, noble, ancient and intimidating without becoming monstrous, demonic or cartoonish. The silver should look medieval and slightly worn rather than polished modern chrome. The fur can be stylized into strong metallic locks or heraldic feather-like plates, but the anatomy must still read clearly as a wolf.

Use the deep saturated blue established for House Aethros: dark, rich, sober, royal, not bright electric blue.

Important:
- no shield frame
- no border
- no text
- no crown
- no swords
- no decorative stars
- no invented secondary symbols
- no background landscape
- no modern logo aesthetic

This image is the MASTER EMBLEM from which banners, military shields, seals and other Aethros heraldic applications will be derived.

Result target:
A frontal silver wolf head on deep Aethros blue, immediately recognizable, severe, medieval, noble, and reusable as the single canonical heraldic emblem of House Aethros.
```
