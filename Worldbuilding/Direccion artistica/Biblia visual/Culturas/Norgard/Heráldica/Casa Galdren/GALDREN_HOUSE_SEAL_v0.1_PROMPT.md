The only heraldic symbol present on both the wax seal and the metal matrix must be the canonical bull of House Galdren.

Result target:
A convincing medieval noble seal and wax impression using the canonical Galdren bull, presented with the same grounded, realistic visual language as the Edranor seal reference: powerful, old, practical, prosperous and unmistakably Galdren.
