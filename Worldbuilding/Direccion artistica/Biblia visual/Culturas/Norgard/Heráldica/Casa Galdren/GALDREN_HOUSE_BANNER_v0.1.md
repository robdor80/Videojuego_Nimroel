## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical cloth banner of House Galdren for Nimroel, derived directly from the approved GALDREN_HERALDIC_EMBLEM_v0.1.

Use the exact same dark-iron heraldic bull design as the master emblem. Place it prominently on a vertical hanging banner made from heavy old wheat-gold medieval fabric.

Use the approved Edranor banner as visual inspiration for banner type, proportions, suspension system, realism, fabric behaviour and degree of wear, while preserving Galdren's own heraldic identity.

The banner must look physically real and preindustrial:
- thick woven cloth
- visible stitching
- folds and weight
- clear signs of age and use
- scratches, stains and weathering
- modest to noticeable fraying near the lower edge
- realistic suspension from a dark aged metal horizontal pole

The bull must remain dark iron / blackened steel and preserve its canonical Galdren identity:
- full heraldic bull in profile
- broad, muscular and powerful body
- one foreleg raised
- large sweeping horns
- proud, grounded and imposing posture
- immediately recognizable silhouette

The old wheat-gold must match the canonical Galdren colour: warm, earthy, rich and sober, closer to aged grain, dry straw or old gold than to bright yellow.

The banner may use restrained dark edging or side bands if they remain consistent with Galdren's black-iron and wheat-gold palette, but no new heraldic motifs may be introduced.

Do not redesign the bull. Do not introduce additional heraldry.

Important:
- no random secondary symbols
- no text
- no slogans
- no crown
- no wheat stalks
- no tools
- no geometric invented emblems
- no excessive decoration
- no modern synthetic fabric
- no pristine ceremonial perfection

This banner is the single canonical Galdren banner design.

Result target:
A worn but prestigious medieval noble banner carrying the exact canonical dark-iron bull of House Galdren on old wheat-gold cloth, physically plausible, powerful, grounded and immediately recognizable.
```
