## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical heraldic emblem of House Galdren for Nimroel.

Use the approved House Edranor heraldic emblem as visual inspiration for overall presentation and compositional logic, but create a distinct emblem for House Galdren.

The emblem must show ONLY a single powerful heraldic bull, centered and dominant, rendered in aged black iron or darkened steel over an old wheat-gold background.

The bull must be shown as a full heraldic creature in profile, proud, heavy, muscular and immediately recognizable. It should feel strong, fertile, grounded, ancient and noble. The design must communicate agricultural abundance, territorial strength, resilience and old lineage, without becoming monstrous, demonic or cartoonish.

The dark metal of the bull should look medieval and slightly worn rather than polished modern metal. The animal can have layered stylized fur around the neck and shoulders, a broad chest, strong limbs and large horns, but the silhouette must remain clear, iconic and highly readable.

The horns are one of the key identity elements. They must be large, sweeping and highly recognizable, giving the bull a commanding and imposing presence.

The pose should suggest contained power and noble solidity, with one foreleg raised in a proud heraldic stance.

Use the old wheat-gold background established for House Galdren: warm, earthy, rich and sober, closer to aged grain, dry straw or old gold than to bright yellow.

Important:
- no shield frame
- no border
- no text
- no crown
- no wheat stalks
- no tools
- no swords
- no decorative stars
- no invented secondary symbols
- no background landscape
- no modern logo aesthetic

This image is the MASTER EMBLEM from which banners, military shields, seals and other Galdren heraldic applications will be derived.

Result target:
A single heraldic bull in aged black iron over old wheat-gold, immediately recognizable, noble, medieval, powerful and reusable as the single canonical heraldic emblem of House Galdren.
```
