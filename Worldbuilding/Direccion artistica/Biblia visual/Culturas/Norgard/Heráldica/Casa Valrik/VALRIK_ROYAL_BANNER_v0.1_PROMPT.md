## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical cloth banner of House Valrik for Nimroel, derived directly from the approved VALRIK_HERALDIC_EMBLEM_v0.1.

Use the exact same aged-bronze sea eagle / noble raptor design as the master emblem. Place it prominently on a vertical hanging banner made from heavy deep forest-green medieval fabric.

Use the approved Galdren banner as visual inspiration for banner type, proportions, suspension system, realism, fabric behaviour and degree of wear, while preserving Valrik's own heraldic identity.

The banner must look physically real and preindustrial:
- thick woven cloth
- visible stitching
- folds and weight
- clear signs of age and use
- scratches, stains and weathering
- noticeable but plausible fraying near the lower edge
- realistic suspension from a dark aged metal horizontal pole

The eagle must remain aged bronze and preserve its canonical Valrik identity:
- full heraldic sea eagle / noble raptor in profile
- proud, vigilant posture
- one wing partially raised
- powerful hooked beak
- strong talons
- layered feather structure
- immediately recognizable silhouette

The deep forest-green must match the canonical Valrik colour: dark, rich, sober and natural, closer to old woodland green or conifer-shadow green than to bright emerald.

The banner may use restrained aged-bronze or dark-gold edging if it remains consistent with Valrik's bronze-and-forest-green palette, but no new heraldic motifs may be introduced.

Do not redesign the eagle. Do not introduce additional heraldry.

Important:
- no random secondary symbols
- no text
- no slogans
- no crown
- no ships
- no fish
- no trees
- no axes
- no geometric invented emblems
- no excessive decoration
- no modern synthetic fabric
- no pristine ceremonial perfection

This banner is the single canonical Valrik banner design.

Result target:
A worn but prestigious medieval noble banner carrying the exact canonical aged-bronze sea eagle of House Valrik on deep forest-green cloth, physically plausible, honorable, vigilant and immediately recognizable.
```
