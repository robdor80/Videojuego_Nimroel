## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical noble sealing system of House Valrik for Nimroel, visually inspired by the approved House Galdren seal composition but replacing all Galdren heraldry with the canonical Valrik sea eagle.

Derive the seal directly from VALRIK_HERALDIC_EMBLEM_v0.1: the same noble sea eagle / heraldic raptor in aged bronze must remain clearly recognizable when simplified into a seal relief.

Show:
- a medieval metal seal matrix or signet stamp carrying the Valrik sea eagle
- a deep forest-green wax seal impressed onto aged parchment or a formal document
- the same eagle clearly visible in the wax impression
- the metal stamp placed nearby, showing the matching engraved eagle design
- physically believable medieval materials, age and wear
- a dark forest-green noble cloth or textile element in the background, consistent with House Valrik colours
- braided sealing cord crossing or securing the parchment, using restrained green and bronze tones

The composition should closely follow the visual language of the canonical Galdren seal reference:
- intimate close-up
- aged parchment document
- sealing cord
- wax impression in the foreground
- metal seal matrix visible nearby
- dark noble textile in the background
- warm cinematic lighting
- realistic medieval still-life presentation

The wax must look real, slightly irregular, used and handcrafted rather than perfectly manufactured. It should use the canonical Valrik deep forest-green tone, dark, sober and natural rather than bright emerald.

The metal matrix should look old, prestigious and functional, made from worn bronze, brass or darkened metal suitable for an honorable and powerful noble house.

The eagle should remain proud, vigilant, disciplined and immediately recognizable. Its hooked beak, partially raised wing, layered feathers and strong talons are essential to the identity of House Valrik and must remain prominent in both the wax impression and the metal matrix.

Fine feather and anatomical details may be simplified where necessary for a believable stamped impression, but the identity, posture and silhouette of the canonical Valrik eagle must be preserved.

Important:

- no letters

- no mottos
- no inscriptions
- no runes
- no invented writing
- no crown
- no ships
- no fish
- no trees
- no axes
- no fleur-de-lis
- no secondary heraldic symbols
- no decorative emblems unrelated to the eagle
- no modern typography
- no redesign of the eagle

The only heraldic symbol present on both the wax seal and the metal matrix must be the canonical sea eagle of House Valrik.

Result target:
A convincing medieval noble seal and wax impression using the canonical Valrik sea eagle, presented with the same grounded, realistic visual language as the Galdren seal reference: honorable, old, practical, vigilant and unmistakably Valrik.
```
