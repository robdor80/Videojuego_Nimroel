## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical military shield used by House Valrik in Nimroel.

Derive the central heraldic device directly from VALRIK_HERALDIC_EMBLEM_v0.1: the same single sea eagle or noble raptor rendered in aged bronze over a deep dark forest-green shield face.

Use the approved Galdren military shield as visual inspiration for shield type, realism and overall presentation, but adapt all heraldry, colours and identity to House Valrik.

The shield must be physically plausible for a preindustrial medieval army:
- strong wooden core
- deep dark forest-green painted or covered face
- aged iron or steel rim
- visible use, scratches, impacts and weathering
- robust construction suitable for soldiers, not decorative wall art

The eagle emblem should be readable and centered, but integrated into the shield surface rather than appearing as a modern 3D logo.

The eagle must preserve the canonical Valrik identity:
- full heraldic sea eagle / noble raptor in profile
- proud, vigilant posture
- partially raised wing
- powerful hooked beak
- strong talons
- aged bronze finish
- immediately recognizable silhouette

Maintain the honorable, disciplined and maritime-woodland identity of House Valrik. This is military equipment, not luxury ceremonial sculpture.

Important:
- exact canonical Valrik eagle
- no alternate bird poses
- no ships
- no fish
- no trees
- no axes
- no random geometric symbols
- no text
- no religious iconography
- no excessive ornament
- no fantasy spikes
- no impractical embellishment
- no modern precision finish

Result target:
A functional, weathered medieval military shield bearing the canonical Valrik aged-bronze eagle on deep forest-green, suitable for troops or retainers of House Valrik.
```
