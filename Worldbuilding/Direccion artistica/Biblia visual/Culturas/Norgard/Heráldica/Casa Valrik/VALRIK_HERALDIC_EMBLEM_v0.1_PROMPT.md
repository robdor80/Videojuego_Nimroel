## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical heraldic emblem of House Valrik for Nimroel.

Use the approved House Galdren heraldic emblem as visual inspiration for overall presentation and compositional logic, but create a distinct emblem for House Valrik.

The emblem must show ONLY a single sea eagle or noble raptor, centered and dominant, rendered in aged bronze over a deep dark forest-green background.

The bird must be shown as a full heraldic creature in profile, proud, vigilant, noble and immediately recognizable. It should feel honorable, ancient, disciplined and strong. The design must communicate loyalty, maritime vigilance, woodland identity and old lineage, without becoming monstrous, demonic or cartoonish.

The bronze should look medieval and slightly worn rather than polished modern metal. The eagle can have layered stylized feathers, a strong hooked beak, a sharp eye, a partially raised wing, powerful talons and a clear, elegant silhouette.

The pose should suggest vigilance and contained strength rather than wild aggression. One talon may be slightly raised in a heraldic stance, and the wing posture should feel dignified and recognizable.

Use the deep forest-green background established for House Valrik: dark, rich, sober and natural, closer to old woodland green or conifer-shadow green than to bright emerald.

Important:
- no shield frame
- no border
- no text
- no crown
- no ships
- no fish
- no trees
- no axes
- no decorative stars
- no invented secondary symbols
- no background landscape
- no modern logo aesthetic

This image is the MASTER EMBLEM from which banners, military shields, seals and other Valrik heraldic applications will be derived.

Result target:
A single heraldic sea eagle in aged bronze on deep forest-green, immediately recognizable, noble, medieval, vigilant and reusable as the single canonical heraldic emblem of House Valrik.
```
