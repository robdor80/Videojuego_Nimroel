- visible stitching
- folds and weight

- modest fraying near the lower edge

Do not redesign the stag. Do not introduce additional heraldry.

Important:
- no random secondary symbols
- no text
- no slogans
- no crown
- no grapes
- no geometric invented emblems
- no excessive decoration
- no modern synthetic fabric
- no pristine ceremonial perfection

This banner is the single canonical Edranor banner design.

Result target:
A worn but prestigious medieval noble banner carrying the exact canonical aged-silver stag of House Edranor on deep wine-purple cloth, physically plausible, aristocratic and immediately recognizable.
