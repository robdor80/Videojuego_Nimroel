## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical military shield used by House Edranor in Nimroel.

Derive the central heraldic device directly from EDRANOR_HERALDIC_EMBLEM_v0.1: the same single noble stag rendered in aged silver over deep Edranor wine-purple.

The shield must be physically plausible for a preindustrial medieval army:
- strong wooden core
- deep wine-purple painted or covered face
- aged iron or steel rim
- visible use, scratches, impacts and weathering
- robust construction suitable for soldiers, not decorative wall art

The stag emblem should be readable and centered, but integrated into the shield surface rather than appearing as a modern 3D logo.

The stag must preserve the canonical Edranor identity:
- full heraldic stag in profile
- proud, upright posture
- one foreleg raised
- large, elegant, branching antlers
- aged silver finish
- immediately recognizable silhouette

Maintain the sober, noble and ambitious visual identity of House Edranor. This is military equipment, not luxury ceremonial sculpture.

Important:
- exact canonical Edranor stag
- no alternate stag poses
- no grapes
- no vines
- no random geometric symbols
- no text
- no religious iconography
- no excessive ornament
- no fantasy spikes
- no impractical embellishment
- no modern precision finish

Result target:
A functional, weathered medieval military shield bearing the canonical Edranor aged-silver stag on deep wine-purple, suitable for troops or retainers of House Edranor.
```
