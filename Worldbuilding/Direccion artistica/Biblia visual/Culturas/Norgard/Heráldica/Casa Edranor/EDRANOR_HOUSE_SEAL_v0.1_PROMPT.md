## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical noble sealing system of House Edranor for Nimroel, visually inspired by the approved House Darovan seal composition but replacing all Darovan heraldry with the canonical Edranor stag.

Derive the seal directly from EDRANOR_HERALDIC_EMBLEM_v0.1: the same noble silver stag with large branching antlers must remain clearly recognizable when simplified into a seal relief.

Show:
- a medieval metal seal matrix or signet stamp carrying the Edranor stag
- a deep wine-purple / burgundy wax seal impressed onto aged parchment or a formal document
- the same stag clearly visible in the wax impression
- the metal stamp placed nearby, showing the matching engraved stag design
- physically believable medieval materials, age and wear
- a dark wine-purple noble cloth or textile element in the background, consistent with House Edranor colours
- braided sealing cord crossing or securing the parchment

The composition should closely follow the visual language of the canonical Darovan seal reference:
- intimate close-up
- aged parchment document
- sealing cord
- wax impression in the foreground
- metal seal matrix visible nearby
- dark noble textile in the background
- warm cinematic lighting
- realistic medieval still-life presentation

The wax must look real, slightly irregular, used and handcrafted rather than perfectly manufactured. It should be deep burgundy-purple, consistent with the canonical Edranor colour palette.

The metal matrix should look old, prestigious and functional, made from worn bronze, brass or darkened metal suitable for a powerful noble house.

The stag should remain proud, aristocratic, ambitious and immediately recognizable. Its large branching antlers are essential to the identity of House Edranor and must remain prominent in both the wax impression and the metal matrix.

Fine fur and anatomical details may be simplified where necessary for a believable stamped impression, but the identity, posture and antlers of the canonical Edranor stag must be preserved.

Important:
- NO TEXT
- NO LATIN
- no letters
- no words
- no mottos
- no inscriptions
- no runes
- no invented writing
- no crown
- no grapes
- no fleur-de-lis
- no secondary heraldic symbols
- no decorative emblems unrelated to the stag
- no modern typography
- no redesign of the stag

The only heraldic symbol present on both the wax seal and the metal matrix must be the canonical stag of House Edranor.

Result target:
A convincing medieval noble seal and wax impression using the canonical Edranor stag, presented with the same grounded, realistic visual language as the Darovan seal reference: aristocratic, old, practical, ambitious and unmistakably Edranor.
```
