## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical heraldic emblem of House Edranor for Nimroel.

Use House Darovan’s approved heraldic emblem as visual inspiration for overall presentation and compositional logic, but create a distinct emblem for House Edranor.

The emblem must show ONLY a single stag, rendered as a noble heraldic animal, centered and dominant, in aged silver over a deep dark wine-purple background.

The stag must be shown as a full heraldic creature in profile, proud and immediately recognizable. It should feel noble, ambitious, ancient and territorial. The design must communicate aristocratic prestige, ambition and old lineage, without becoming monstrous, demonic or cartoonish.

The silver should look medieval and slightly worn rather than polished modern metal. The stag can have layered stylized fur and a strong sculpted body, but the silhouette must remain clear and iconic.

The antlers are one of the key identity elements. They must be large, elegant, branching and highly recognizable, giving the stag a commanding and almost crown-like presence without using any literal crown.

Use the deep saturated wine-purple established for House Edranor: dark, rich, sober, noble, closer to a royal wine tone than to bright violet.

Important:
- no shield frame
- no border
- no text
- no crown
- no grapes
- no swords
- no decorative stars
- no invented secondary symbols
- no background landscape
- no modern logo aesthetic

This image is the MASTER EMBLEM from which banners, military shields, seals and other Edranor heraldic applications will be derived.

Result target:
A single heraldic stag in aged silver on deep Edranor wine-purple, immediately recognizable, aristocratic, medieval, ambitious and reusable as the single canonical heraldic emblem of House Edranor.
```
