## Prompt operativo

```text
Create the canonical heraldic emblem of House Darovan for Nimroel.

The emblem must show ONLY a single griffin in a classic heraldic rampant pose, centered and dominant, rendered in aged gold over a deep dark crimson background.

The griffin must be shown as a full heraldic creature in profile, powerful and immediately recognizable. It should feel proud, noble, wealthy, ancient and formidable. The design must communicate aristocratic power, economic strength and ambition, without becoming monstrous, demonic or cartoonish.

The gold should look medieval and slightly worn rather than polished modern metal. The griffin can have layered heraldic feathers and a strong sculpted body, but the silhouette must remain clear and iconic.

Use the deep saturated crimson established for House Darovan: dark, rich, sober, noble, closer to carmine or royal burgundy than to bright red.

Important:
- no shield frame
- no border
- no text
- no crown
- no coins
- no swords
- no decorative stars
- no invented secondary symbols
- no background landscape
- no modern logo aesthetic

This image is the MASTER EMBLEM from which banners, military shields, seals and other Darovan heraldic applications will be derived.

Result target:
A single rampant griffin in aged gold on deep Darovan crimson, immediately recognizable, aristocratic, medieval, wealthy and reusable as the single canonical heraldic emblem of House Darovan.
```
