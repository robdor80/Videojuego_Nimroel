Create the canonical cloth banner of House Darovan for Nimroel, derived directly from the approved DAROVAN_HERALDIC_EMBLEM_v0.1.

- visible stitching
- folds and weight

Do not redesign the griffin. Do not introduce additional heraldry.

Important:
- no random secondary symbols
- no text
- no slogans
- no crown
- no coins
- no geometric invented emblems
- no excessive decoration
- no modern synthetic fabric
- no pristine ceremonial perfection

This banner is the single canonical Darovan banner design.

Result target:
A worn but prestigious medieval noble banner carrying the exact canonical rampant aged-gold griffin of House Darovan on deep crimson cloth, physically plausible, aristocratic and immediately recognizable.
